Port Minecraft mods between Fabric, NeoForge and Forge

Drop in a Fabric, NeoForge or Forge mod and pick a loader. An AI rewrites the loader code and builds a fresh jar. Works for Minecraft 1.20.5 and newer, takes 20–40 minutes, and small mods work best.

    Before you start

    Mod Porter is a free fan tool. To use it, you agree that:

    • You only port mods you're allowed to change.
    • A ported mod is not yours. You won't claim it as your work, remove the author's credits, or upload it anywhere as your own.
    • You won't share or sell ported mods unless the original license or author allows it.
    • Ports can be broken. Results are kept for 30 days, and only you (or someone with the exact same file) can download them.

    Keep the furnace burning

    Mod Porter is free and always will be. Every port uses servers and AI time, so if it saved you some work, a small tip helps keep it running.
